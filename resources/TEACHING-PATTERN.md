# Teaching references and adaptation

Reviewed 16 September 2026. This template borrows teaching patterns, not proprietary course content or NVIDIA code.

- [NVIDIA AI Workbench onboarding tutorial](https://github.com/NVIDIA/workbench-example-onboarding-project): visible prerequisites, sequential basic-to-advanced exercises, and checks before moving on. We adapt that into a consistent lab page and explicit checkpoint.
- [NVIDIA instructor-led workshop overview](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/training/instructor-led-workshops/): practical applications, hands-on environments, instructor support, and learner competency. We adapt those principles into prework, a useful shared project, hints, and an evidence-based close.
- [NVIDIA self-paced catalog](https://learn.nvidia.com/en-us/training/self-paced-courses): self-directed technical learning. We make the attendee route usable without an instructor narrating every step.

The 60/90/120-minute agendas are our design, not NVIDIA-prescribed timing. We do not claim NVIDIA endorsement, course equivalence, certification, or a completed classroom pilot.

# Lab 1 — Run it

**15 minutes** · 5 min watch, 10 min you

## You'll build

Nothing yet — you'll get the app running and find the one line where {{QUALCOMM_TECHNOLOGY}} actually does the work.

## Watch first

Your host runs `{{RUN_COMMAND}}` and points at three things: the input, the {{QUALCOMM_TECHNOLOGY}} call in `{{FIRST_RUN_FILE}}`, and the output.

> **In one line:** {{ONE_LINE_EXPLANATION_OF_THE_TECHNOLOGY}} — more detail: {{OFFICIAL_DOC_LINK}}

## Your turn

1. Run `{{RUN_COMMAND}}`.
2. Open `{{FIRST_RUN_FILE}}` and find the {{QUALCOMM_TECHNOLOGY}} call. Say out loud what goes in and what comes out.
3. Change the input to `{{ALTERNATE_INPUT_EXAMPLE}}` and run it again.
4. Before you look at the result — guess what changes and what stays the same. Then check.

## Done when

You've seen two different outputs from two different inputs, and you can point at the line that produced them.

## Go further

- What part of this runs on your device and what part needs the network? Find evidence in the code, don't guess.
- Give it deliberately bad input. What happens?

## Stuck?

1. Are you in the right folder with the environment active? Re-run the setup check: `{{READINESS_COMMAND}}`.
2. Read the first line of the error, not the last. It usually names the file.
3. Still stuck: `{{FIRST_RUN_RECOVERY}}` — then grab the host.

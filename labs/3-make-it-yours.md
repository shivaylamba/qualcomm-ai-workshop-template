# Lab 3 — Make it yours

**25 minutes** · all yours, the host circulates

## You'll build

One change that makes this useful for *you* — your data, your output format, or your feature.

## Your turn

1. **Pick one** (10 seconds, don't overthink):
   - Feed it your own data instead of the sample
   - Change the output into something you'd actually use ({{OUTPUT_FORMAT_IDEAS}})
   - Add one small feature: {{EXTENSION_IDEAS}}
2. Write down what "it works" means before you build it. One sentence.
3. Build it in `{{EXTENSION_FILE}}`. Keep the same model and runtime — change one layer only.
4. Re-run `{{BUILD_CHECK_COMMAND}}` to make sure you didn't break Lab 2.
5. Hand your laptop to someone else and let them try an input you didn't expect.

## Done when

Someone else ran your version and got a result — or you can explain exactly why it didn't work.

## Go further

- Make it handle the input that just broke it.
- Write down the one thing you'd need before anyone else could use this.

## Stuck?

1. Your change is too big. Cut it in half.
2. Revert to your Lab 2 version and change one thing at a time.
3. Out of time? Show your plan and your test case instead. That counts.

## Your 60-second demo

> Here's what it does → here's the line I changed → here's what still doesn't work.

Don't demo someone else's screenshot. A broken thing you understand is worth more than a working thing you copied.
